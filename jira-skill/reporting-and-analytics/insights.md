# Insights & Analytics

## What Are Insights?

Jira Insights are "data-driven decision" tools enabling teams to "analyze performance without leaving workflow context."

The system aggregates historical progress data from boards and projects, currently supporting Scrum metrics across company-managed and team-managed structures.

## Backlog Insights

### Sprint Commitment Insight
**Purpose**: Evaluate workload distribution and planning accuracy

**Metrics**:
- Performance data from last 5 sprints
- Overcommitted vs. undercommitted indicators
- Comparison against calculated targets
- Estimation accuracy

**Use**: Improve sprint planning accuracy

### Issue Type Breakdown Insight
**Purpose**: Visibility into work composition

**Metrics**:
- Bug vs. feature vs. technical debt distribution
- Work category balance
- Organizational priority alignment

**Use**: Ensure work types match organizational priorities

## Board Insights

### Sprint Progress Insight
**Purpose**: Real-time sprint completion tracking

**Metrics**:
- Completion percentage by status
- Items by workflow stage
- Daily progress trends

**Use**: Rapid standup updates without detailed reports

### Burndown Insight
**Purpose**: Sprint trajectory and completion likelihood

**Metrics**:
- Completed work vs. remaining
- Current completion rate
- Projected completion date

**Use**: Early warning of sprint issues

### Issues That Need Attention Insight
**Purpose**: Rapid identification of blockers

**Surfaced Issues**:
- Blocked items (dependencies blocking)
- Stuck items (no progress recently)
- Flagged items (team-marked priority)

**Use**: Daily standup blocker identification

### Epic Progress Insight
**Purpose**: Link sprint work to strategic goals

**Metrics**:
- Epic completion percentage
- Work items advancing epic
- Sprint contribution to epic

**Use**: Strategic alignment visibility

## How to Access Insights

1. Navigate to your board (Scrum or Kanban)
2. Click desired insight
3. View interactive data
4. Click for detailed view

## Insights Best Practices

1. **Regular Monitoring**: Review insights during sprint ceremonies
2. **Team Discussion**: Discuss patterns and trends
3. **Trend Analysis**: Look for improvement patterns
4. **Action on Alerts**: Respond to attention-needed items
5. **Process Improvement**: Adjust based on insights

## Audit Applications

**Compliance Tracking**:
- Sprint commitment compliance
- Burndown adherence
- Work item categorization
- Blocker identification
- Escalation evidence

**Capacity Planning**:
- Velocity trends (do we have capacity?)
- Commitment accuracy (are we realistic?)
- Blocker frequency (what's blocking us?)

## Limitations

**Current Support**: Scrum metrics only (not Kanban-specific insights)

**Future**: Expect expansion of insight types and reporting
