# What is Jira?

## Definition

Jira is an **industry-leading project management platform** that brings teams together to "plan, track, and deliver any type of project with confidence." It functions as a "single source of truth for your entire organization."

## Core Value Proposition

**Unified Platform**: One centralized system for all work tracking across teams and departments.

**Flexibility**: Customizable to support any project methodology—Agile, Waterfall, Hybrid, or custom processes.

**Extensibility**: 3,000+ apps and integrations available through Atlassian Marketplace.

**AI-Powered**: Rovo AI integration for intelligent workflow automation and collaboration.

**Built-In Compliance**: Security, privacy, and compliance capabilities included in all editions.

## Market Adoption

- **300,000+ companies** globally have adopted Jira
- **Deployable at any scale**: From 2 to 2,000+ team members
- **Cross-industry**: Financial services, retail, software, high-tech, automotive, nonprofits, government agencies, life sciences

## Who Uses Jira?

**Development Teams**: Code tracking, CI/CD integration, deployment management

**Marketing Teams**: Campaign management, initiative alignment, timeline coordination

**Design Teams**: Collaboration, asset tracking, workflow integration

**IT Operations**: Ticket management, change control, service delivery

**Project Management**: Multi-team coordination, resource allocation, timeline tracking

**Government Agencies**: Compliance tracking, audit documentation, process control

## Key Differentiators

1. **Agile Ready**: Built-in Scrum and Kanban boards
2. **Customizable Workflows**: Design processes matching your organization
3. **Permission Control**: Granular access control for governance
4. **Reporting**: Out-of-the-box analytics and custom dashboards
5. **Automation**: No-code automation rules for repetitive tasks
6. **Integration Ecosystem**: Connect 3,000+ tools and applications

## Jira's Role in Organizations

Jira serves as:
- **Work Tracking System** - Unified view of all organizational work
- **Process Enforcer** - Workflows ensure consistent execution
- **Compliance Engine** - Audit trails and permission controls
- **Communication Hub** - Centralized visibility for distributed teams
- **Analytics Platform** - Data-driven decision making
