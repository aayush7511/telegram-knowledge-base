## Key Takeaways

- **Jira** is an industry-leading project management platform adopted by 300,000+ companies globally
- **Core Elements**: Spaces, Work Items, People, and Workflows
- **Three Editions**: Free (10 users), Standard, Premium (Enterprise)
- **Built for Everyone**: Software teams, marketing, design, IT operations, government agencies

## Common Questions

**Q: Is Jira only for software development?**  
A: No. While originally built for developers, Jira now serves marketing, design, IT operations, project management, and government compliance teams.

**Q: Which edition should we choose?**  
A: Start with Free (testing), move to Standard (mid-sized teams), or Premium (enterprise scale and advanced features).

**Q: Can we migrate between editions?**  
A: Yes. You can upgrade anytime; downgrading may have implications on advanced features.
