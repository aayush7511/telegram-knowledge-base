# Jira Editions Overview

## Three Editions Available

Atlassian offers three distinct Jira editions optimized for different organizational scales and requirements.

### Free Edition

**Target**: Small teams and proof-of-concept testing

**User Capacity**: Up to 10 users at no cost

**What's Included**:
- Access to "almost every feature"
- 1,000+ app integrations from Atlassian Marketplace
- Built-in security, privacy, and compliance features
- Community support via forums

**Best For**:
- Testing Jira before larger investment
- Small teams with limited budget
- Departments exploring agile workflows
- Non-profit organizations

**Cost**: $0/month

### Standard Edition

**Target**: Small to mid-sized organizations

**User Capacity**: Unlimited users (pricing per user)

**What's Included**:
- All Free edition features
- Increased storage capacity
- Business hours support (Atlassian support team)
- Advanced integrations and configurations
- Atlassian Solutions Partner access

**Best For**:
- Growing teams exceeding 10 users
- Mid-sized organizations with standard needs
- Teams requiring business-hours support
- Organizations standardizing on Jira

**Cost**: Scales with number of users

### Premium Edition

**Target**: Enterprise and rapidly scaling organizations

**User Capacity**: Unlimited users

**What's Included**:
- All Standard features
- **Unlimited storage**
- **24/7 support** (response time: under 1 hour SLA)
- **99.9% uptime guarantee**
- Advanced features (Advanced Planning, enhanced automation)
- Dedicated account management
- Custom configurations and integrations

**Best For**:
- Enterprise organizations
- High-availability requirements
- Complex multi-team environments
- Organizations requiring advanced features

**Cost**: Contact Atlassian for pricing

## Edition Comparison Matrix

| Feature | Free | Standard | Premium |
|---------|------|----------|---------|
| User Limit | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage | Limited | Increased | Unlimited |
| Support | Community | Business Hours | 24/7 |
| Uptime SLA | None | Standard | 99.9% |
| Advanced Features | Limited | Included | Enhanced |
| Compliance Features | Basic | Standard | Enhanced |

## Compliance & Security (All Editions)

**All editions include industry-leading security, privacy, and compliance features**:
- Data encryption (at rest and in transit)
- Access controls and permissions
- Audit trails and logging
- Compliance certifications

For government use, ensure your organization verifies Atlassian's compliance certifications (SOC 2, FedRAMP if applicable).

## Choosing Your Edition

**Decision Framework**:
1. **Start with Free** if testing or small team
2. **Move to Standard** when exceeding 10 users
3. **Upgrade to Premium** for enterprise scale or advanced features

**Upgrade Path**: You can upgrade anytime without data loss. Downgrading may impact advanced features.
