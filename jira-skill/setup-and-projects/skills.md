## Key Concepts

**Team-Managed Spaces**
- Self-contained team control
- No IT involvement required
- Ideal for independent teams
- Simplified configuration

**Company-Managed Spaces**
- Standardized across organization
- Centralized governance
- Advanced features (Advanced Planning, detailed reporting)
- Multi-team coordination

## Organizational Models

1. **Team-Based**: One space per department
2. **Business Unit**: One space per division
3. **Product-Based**: One space per product/product family

**Important**: There is no one-size-fits-all approach. Choose based on your organizational structure.

## Common Setup Decisions

**Decision Tree**:
- Small/independent team? → Team-managed
- Multiple teams, need consistency? → Company-managed
- Government/compliance requirements? → Company-managed (better audit control)
- Need Advanced Planning? → Company-managed (Premium edition)
